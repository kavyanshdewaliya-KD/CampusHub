CampusHub — Privacy Notice

Policy Version: v1.0-2026-09-07 Last Updated: [DATE]

This Privacy Notice explains how CampusHub ("we", "the app") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data, in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

CampusHub is a batch-management tool built for IET DAVV CSE-B. By using the app, you are a "Data Principal" under the DPDP Act, and we are the "Data Fiduciary" responsible for your personal data.

1. What Data We Collect, and Why

We only collect data needed to run the app for you and your batch. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

Data We Collect Where It Comes From Why We Collect It (Purpose)
Name, email address, profile photo Your Google Sign-In To identify you to your batchmates, show your name on chats/polls, and let CRs manage batch access
Role (Student / CR / Admin) Set by a Class Representative To control who can post announcements, approve members, and moderate chat
Attendance records you mark You, inside the Attendance tab To calculate your personal subject-wise attendance percentage — this data is private to your account
Chat messages, discussion topics, poll votes You, when you use Chat / Discuss / Polls To power batch-wide and private conversations, and to run class polls
Device push-notification token (FCM token) Your browser/device, if you allow notifications To send you notices about new announcements, approved discussions, etc.
IP address, browser/device info Automatically, when you record consent to this notice To maintain a verifiable record that you consented to this notice (required under Section 6 of the DPDP Act)
Online/offline status Automatically, while the app is open To show batchmates who's currently active (presence indicator)

We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 18 without verifiable parental/guardian consent as required by the DPDP Act. If you believe a minor has used this app, please contact the Grievance Officer below.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate core features: timetable, attendance tracking, assignments, announcements, resources, chat, polls, and discussion topics.
  • To let Class Representatives/Admins manage batch membership and approve access requests.
  • To send you notifications about updates relevant to you.
  • To maintain a legally required record of your consent (see Section 6, DPDP Act).

We do not use your data for any purpose beyond what's listed above without asking you again.

3. Who Can See Your Data

  • Your name, role, and messages you post publicly (Class Chat, approved Discussion topics, Polls) are visible to other approved members of your batch.
  • Private chats are visible only to you and the specific person you're messaging.
  • Your attendance records are private to your account only — no other student can see them. CRs/Admins do not have access to individual attendance logs unless explicitly built into a feature and disclosed to you separately.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for marketing or advertising purposes.
  • Data may be processed by our infrastructure providers (Google Firebase, Vercel) strictly as service providers to run the app — they do not use your data for their own purposes.

4. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • We retain your data for as long as you have an active account.
  • If you delete your account (see Section 6 below), we erase or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we are legally required or permitted to retain limited records (for example, proof of consent) for audit and compliance purposes.

5. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  1. Right to Access (Section 11): Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you, in a clear, accessible format.
  2. Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12): Ask us to correct inaccurate data, or delete your account and associated personal data entirely.
  3. Right to Withdraw Consent (Section 6): Withdraw your consent to processing at any time. Withdrawing consent may mean you can no longer use parts (or all) of the app that depend on that data.
  4. Right to Grievance Redressal (Section 13): Raise a complaint about how your data is handled — see below.
  5. Right to Nominate: In the event of your death or incapacity, nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf, by contacting the Grievance Officer.

To exercise these rights, use the "Export My Data" or "Delete My Account" options inside the app's Settings, or contact the Grievance Officer below directly.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards including authenticated access (Google Sign-In), encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), and access-controlled cloud infrastructure (Google Firebase) to protect your data from unauthorised access, alteration, or loss.

7. Changes to This Notice

If we materially change what data we collect or how we use it, we will show you an updated consent prompt the next time you open the app, and you'll need to agree again before continuing.

8. Grievance Redressal (Section 13, DPDP Act)

If you have any concerns, questions, or complaints about how your personal data is collected or processed, please contact our Grievance Officer:

Grievance Officer: [YOUR NAME] Designation: Class Representative / Grievance Officer, CampusHub — IET DAVV CSE-B Email: [YOUR-EMAIL@example.com] Response Timeline: We aim to acknowledge and resolve all grievances within 30 days of receipt.

If you are not satisfied with our resolution, you may escalate your complaint to the Data Protection Board of India, once it is fully operational, via the process notified by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY).
