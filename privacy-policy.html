3. Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data on the basis of your explicit, informed consent, given via the consent screen shown before sign-in (DPDP Act, Section 6). You may withdraw this consent at any time (see Section 6 below) — withdrawal doesn't affect processing already carried out before you withdrew it, and may mean you can no longer use parts of the app that need that data.

4. Who we share data with

We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers. Your data is processed by these service providers, strictly to run the app:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore database, Realtime Database, Cloud Messaging) — our core infrastructure. Firebase may store and process data on servers located outside India.
  • Vercel — hosts our web app and backend functions, and may process data on servers located outside India.
Cross-border transfer note: Under Section 16 of the DPDP Act, cross-border transfer of personal data is permitted by default unless the Central Government specifically restricts a country. We currently rely on Google Firebase and Vercel's global infrastructure, which may process data outside India as part of their standard service.

Your class's CR/Admin users can see profile and content data needed to moderate the class space (e.g., chat messages, discussion requests) — never your private attendance records or calendar plans, which are visible only to you.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Account & profile data: for as long as your account is active.
  • Consent logs: retained for [e.g., 3 years] after account closure, solely as proof of consent, as required for legal accountability.
  • If you delete your account, personal data is deleted or anonymized as described in Section 7 below, typically within [e.g., 30 days].

6. Your rights under the DPDP Act

  • Right to Access (Section 11): Request a summary of the personal data we hold about you. In-app: ⚙️ Settings → Export My Data.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12): Correct inaccurate data, or request deletion of your account and personal data. In-app: ⚙️ Settings → Delete My Account & Data.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (Section 6): Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account, or by emailing our Grievance Officer below.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal (Section 13): Raise a complaint about how your data is handled — see Section 8 below.
  • Right to Nominate (Section 14): Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity, by emailing our Grievance Officer.

7. What happens when you delete your account

When you request deletion, we permanently delete your Firebase Auth account, your private attendance and calendar data, your poll votes, and your private/discussion chat messages. Content you posted in shared class spaces (like chat or discussion topics) that others may still be relying on is anonymized (your name is replaced with "Deleted User") rather than removed outright, so ongoing class conversations aren't broken for other members.

9. Children's data

No system is 100% secure, and we encourage you to report any suspected vulnerability to our Grievance Officer.
